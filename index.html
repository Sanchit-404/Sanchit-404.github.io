About Me

Hi!

I'm a PhD student in Computer Science at Virginia Tech, advised by Prof. Chandan Reddy. My research interests are centered around self evolving agents and their applications for scientific discovery, security of LLM systems and openendedness.

Before starting my Ph.D., I completed my undergraduate degree in Computer Science at BITS Pilani, India.

Research Interests

My research studies how LLM-based agents can sustain progress in complex settings that require continual exploration rather than fast convergence. I design systems that generate hypotheses, evaluate their own failures, and update their strategies at test time using programmatic feedback loops and structured evaluation.

I am particularly interested in:

  • Scientific discovery, where agents iteratively refine hypotheses, design experiments or queries, and adapt search strategies under feasibility and budget constraints.
  • Red teaming of LLM systems, where agents must diversify attacks, reason about defenses, and adapt to changing policies and evaluators while operating under strict query budgets.
  • Open-ended behavior, where maintaining novelty, diversity, and adaptability over long horizons is essential, and where premature convergence and reward exploitation are core failure modes.
